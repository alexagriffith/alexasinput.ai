About the Show

Alexa Griffith is a field deployment engineer at Red Hat, working hands-on with customers deploying AI and ML workloads in production. This podcast brings together her unique perspective from the field with conversations from builders, founders, and technical leaders.

Each episode goes beneath the surface to understand the mechanisms — exploring not just what works, but why it works, and what breaks in production.
